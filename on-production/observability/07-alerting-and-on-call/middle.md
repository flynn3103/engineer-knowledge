# Alerting and On-Call — Middle

<!-- level-focus -->
At middle level, design alerts and runbooks that support a team instead of creating noise.

## Method

Page for urgent user impact; route tickets for work that can wait. Every page needs an owner, severity, dashboard, runbook, and escalation policy. Group related symptoms and tune with incident evidence.

## Apply it

1. Convert one noisy page into a ticket or multi-window alert.
2. Test its runbook with a teammate.

## Verify your work

- The page has a specific response action.
- Handoff includes current evidence and owner.

## Review questions

- What makes an alert actionable?
- When should an alert not page?
