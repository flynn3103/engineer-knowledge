# Alerting and On-Call — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, create a sustainable on-call operating model across teams.

## Method

Set workload, training, escalation, and post-incident improvement expectations. Measure page volume, acknowledgement time, sleep disruption, repeat incidents, and runbook success. Fund systemic fixes rather than normalizing heroics.

## Apply it

1. Review rotation load and escalation gaps.
2. Convert a recurring page into an owned improvement.

## Verify your work

- Teams have capacity to respond and improve.
- On-call measures drive prioritization.

## Review questions

- What evidence signals an unsustainable rotation?
- How should repeat pages change planning?
