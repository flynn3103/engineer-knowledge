# Observability — Senior

Design telemetry for partial failure, retries, queue age, data correctness, and recovery. Use SLOs and error-budget burn to focus alerting. Manage cost through aggregation, tiered retention, adaptive sampling, and per-team budgets without eliminating healthy comparison data.

Treat collectors and pipelines as production systems with backpressure, dropped-data metrics, tenant isolation, and disaster behavior.

## Test yourself

1. Which signal proves correctness, not only availability?
2. How do you retain rare failures economically?
3. What happens when collectors back up?
4. Which telemetry validates recovery?
