# Alerting and On-Call — Senior

<!-- level-focus -->
At senior level, shape alerting around system risk, recovery, and ownership boundaries.

## Method

Alert on SLO burn and resource exhaustion before cascading failure. Define incident roles and fallback ownership for dependencies. Exercise a regional failure and verify that alerts reveal impact, not merely component noise.

## Apply it

1. Map pages to customer outcomes.
2. Run a failure drill and revise escalation.

## Verify your work

- Alerts remain useful during a dependency outage.
- Recovery has measurable exit conditions.

## Review questions

- Why can component alerts obscure customer impact?
- Who owns an unresponsive dependency?
