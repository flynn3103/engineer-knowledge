# Metrics Pipelines — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, operate metrics collection as a dependable shared platform with clear cost and quality ownership.

## Deliver a platform capability

Platform owns availability, tenancy, and guardrails; teams own metric semantics and dashboards. Introduce quotas with visibility first, then warnings, then enforcement, while offering migration help. Measure query latency, missing-data incidents, active-series cost, and adoption of standard service metrics.

## Apply it

1. Define tenant responsibilities and escalation.
2. Roll out one quota in reversible stages.
3. Review cost and evidence quality with service owners.

## Verify your work

- Quotas do not silently discard critical telemetry.
- Teams can explain and correct their usage.

## Review questions

- Which pipeline responsibility belongs to a tenant?
- What makes a quota rollout reversible?
