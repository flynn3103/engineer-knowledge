# Log Aggregation — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, operate logging as a governed evidence service across teams.

## Method

Assign platform availability, producer semantics, and security governance to named owners. Roll out schema rules gradually, measure ingestion cost and investigation success, and escalate policy violations to the producing team.

## Apply it

1. Publish ownership and exception paths.
2. Stage a retention-policy migration.

## Verify your work

- Teams can correct violations without losing evidence.

## Review questions

- Who owns a sensitive log field?
- What outcome shows the service is useful?
