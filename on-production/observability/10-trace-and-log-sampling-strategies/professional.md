# Trace and Log Sampling Strategies — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, govern sampling as a shared evidence, cost, and compliance decision.

## Method

Assign policy owners, make incident overrides time-bound, audit access, and review whether retained samples support investigations. Measure cost, error-trace coverage, and time to answer support cases.

## Apply it

1. Approve a temporary incident override.
2. Review its rollback and audit trail.

## Verify your work

- Overrides expire automatically.

## Review questions

- Who may change retention during an incident?
