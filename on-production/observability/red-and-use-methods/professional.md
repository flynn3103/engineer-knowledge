# RED and USE Methods — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, make service and capacity evidence a shared operating practice across teams.

## Standardize without hiding context

Platform supplies RED/USE templates and query libraries; owning teams choose thresholds and document business impact. Roll out by instrumenting the highest-risk journeys, reviewing incidents for coverage gaps, then turning proven fields into standards. Measure page usefulness, time to identify the constrained resource, and unowned saturation alerts.

## Apply it

1. Assign ownership for each service and capacity boundary.
2. Make one template change reversible and test it during an incident drill.
3. Review alert noise and missing-resource evidence quarterly.

## Verify your work

- Templates accelerate investigation without forcing irrelevant panels.
- Capacity risks have named escalation paths.

## Review questions

- What should a central template own versus a service team?
- How would you detect that a USE dashboard is never actionable?
