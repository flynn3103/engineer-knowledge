# SLO, SLI, and Error Budgets — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, use service objectives as a durable agreement among product, platform, and delivery teams.

## Operate the agreement

Agree on customer outcomes, measurement ownership, and what happens when a budget is exhausted. Release policy, incident escalation, and roadmap prioritization must follow the same evidence. Introduce objectives one journey at a time; review missed targets with product and support, then revise either the system or an objectively wrong measure.

Track customer-impacting minutes, budget burn, release reversions, and time to recover. Make exceptions time-bound and named, especially for launch events or compliance work.

## Apply it

1. Assign outcome, measurement, and release-policy owners.
2. Run a budget-exhaustion exercise with a planned rollback.
3. Review whether objectives changed a prioritization decision.

## Verify your work

- Teams know the action tied to each budget state.
- Measures and exceptions are auditable.

## Review questions

- Who resolves a conflict between a launch deadline and a depleted budget?
- Which outcome measure detects an SLO program that is only paperwork?
