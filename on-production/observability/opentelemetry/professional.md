# OpenTelemetry — Professional

<!-- level-focus -->
At professional level, operate OpenTelemetry as a shared compatibility and governance program.

## Method

Platform owns distribution and collectors; teams own semantic evidence. Stage upgrades, publish support windows, and measure connected-flow coverage, cost, and policy violations.

## Apply it

1. Assign upgrade and schema owners.
2. Run a staged migration.

## Verify your work

- Upgrades have rollback paths.

## Review questions

- Who owns convention compatibility?
- What makes migration complete?
