# Cost Efficiency — Professional

FinOps allocates, analyzes, and optimizes shared cloud spend; AWS Savings Plans and reserved capacity exchange flexibility for commitment; Kubernetes bin packing and autoscaling trade utilization against availability; object-store lifecycle policies move data between price and retrieval tiers.

## Design and operations checklist

1. Attribute cost to owner and useful outcome.
2. Preserve reliability, privacy, and security constraints.
3. Forecast demand and commitment risk.
4. Optimize data movement and lifecycle.
5. Account for engineering and operational cost.
6. Verify savings persist without shifted harm.

```text
DEMAND -> RESOURCES -> SHARED COST -> UNIT ECONOMICS -> OPTIMIZE -> VERIFY
```

## Test yourself

1. Design chargeback for a shared data platform.
2. When does high utilization reduce business value?
3. How do commitments fail under forecast error?
4. Which optimization merely shifts cost to another team?
