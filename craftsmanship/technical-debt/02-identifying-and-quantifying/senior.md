# Identifying & Quantifying — Senior

## Goal

Model debt as a changing system rather than a static score.

## Advanced evidence

- Use version history to find churn, change coupling, and abandoned ownership.
- Track complexity trends; acceleration matters more than a single value.
- Combine likelihood of future change with the cost per change.
- Map knowledge concentration and dependencies across teams.

## Present honestly

- Give ranges and assumptions.
- Separate observed cost from forecast cost.
- Revisit the model after repayment to learn whether the estimate was useful.
