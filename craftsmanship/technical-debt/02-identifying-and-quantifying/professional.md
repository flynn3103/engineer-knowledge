# Identifying & Quantifying — Professional

## Goal

Give leadership a credible view of debt without creating vanity metrics.

## Dashboard principles

- Combine code signals with delivery, reliability, and security outcomes.
- Show trends and uncertainty, not a fictional exact debt total.
- Give every material hotspot an owner and decision date.
- Review the dashboard during planning, not only after incidents.

## Protect the measurement

- Explain what each metric cannot prove.
- Check for gaming and unintended incentives.
- Use sampled investigations to validate automated signals.

## Outcome

Fund work where reducing interest unlocks measurable delivery or risk reduction.
