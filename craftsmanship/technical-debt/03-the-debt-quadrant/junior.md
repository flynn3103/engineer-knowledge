# The Debt Quadrant — Junior

## Goal

Choose a response based on how and why debt was created.

## Two axes

- Deliberate or inadvertent.
- Prudent or reckless.

## The four responses

- Deliberate and prudent: record the trade-off and repayment trigger.
- Inadvertent and prudent: learn, improve the design, and share the lesson.
- Deliberate and reckless: escalate the risk and stop repeating it.
- Inadvertent and reckless: add guardrails, training, and review.

## Practice

Classify one recent shortcut without blaming a person. Focus on evidence and the next action.
