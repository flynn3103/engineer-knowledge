# The Debt Quadrant — Professional

## Goal

Make the quadrant a shared policy and planning language.

## Policy

- Record deliberate-prudent debt in an ADR or tracked item.
- Require the benefit, risk, owner, and repayment trigger.
- Use postmortems to identify process causes, not scapegoats.
- Escalate repeated reckless shortcuts as an incentives or capacity problem.

## With product

Explain the available choices, their delivery benefit, and their future cost. Product and engineering should jointly authorize material trade-offs.
