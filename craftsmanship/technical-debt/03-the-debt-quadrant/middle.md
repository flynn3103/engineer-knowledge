# The Debt Quadrant — Middle

## Goal

Use the quadrant to turn vague debt discussions into appropriate action.

## Classify carefully

- Deliberate means the trade-off was known when made.
- Prudent means the choice fit available information and constraints.
- Classification can change as facts emerge; do not rewrite history to protect egos.

## Act by cell

- Track deliberate-prudent debt with an owner and trigger.
- Improve discovery for inadvertent-prudent debt.
- Add review gates and accountability for reckless patterns.

## Limits

The quadrant explains the origin; it does not rank impact. Pair it with hotspots and cost.
