# Paying Down Debt — Junior

## Goal

Improve code safely while delivering normal work.

## The boy scout rule

Leave a small area cleaner than you found it when the improvement is safe and relevant.

## Safe sequence

1. Add or confirm a test around the behavior.
2. Make one small structural change.
3. Run the tests.
4. Keep the cleanup separate from behavior changes.
5. Stop when scope or risk grows.

## Do not pay it now when

- You cannot protect behavior.
- The change is unrelated to urgent work.
- The needed decision or owner is missing.
