# Paying Down Debt — Professional

## Goal

Fund and run repayment work that leadership can understand and sustain.

## Make the case

- State the business capability blocked by the debt.
- Show recurring interest: delay, incidents, support cost, or compliance exposure.
- Break work into deployable milestones with evidence at each milestone.
- Keep product and operations involved in trade-offs and rollout.

## Funding models

- Fix-on-touch for local debt.
- Protected recurring capacity for chronic hotspots.
- Explicit roadmap initiatives for strategic modernization.

Each model needs an owner, outcome, and stop condition.
