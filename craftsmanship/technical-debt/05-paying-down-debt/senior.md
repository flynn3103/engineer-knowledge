# Paying Down Debt — Senior

## Goal

Replace high-risk architecture without stopping the business.

## Incremental replacement

- Put an abstraction in front of the old and new paths.
- Move one capability or traffic slice at a time.
- Use shadow traffic, parallel runs, or dark launches when appropriate.
- Monitor correctness, cost, latency, and rollback readiness.
- Retire the old path only after evidence and ownership are clear.

## Decide on a rewrite

Choose it only when incremental options are exhausted and migration, data, operations, funding, and risk are all planned.
