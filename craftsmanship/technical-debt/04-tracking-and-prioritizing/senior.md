# Tracking & Prioritizing — Senior

## Goal

Allocate scarce capacity where debt reduction creates the most option value.

## Strategy

- Combine hotspot evidence with debt quadrant classification.
- Model expected interest as likelihood of change times added cost and risk.
- Identify keystone debts whose repayment makes several other changes cheaper.
- Set a portfolio budget, then revise it from delivery and incident evidence.

## Avoid

- Severity-only sorting.
- A separate debt list that never enters planning.
- Large paydown programs with no stop condition or outcome metric.
