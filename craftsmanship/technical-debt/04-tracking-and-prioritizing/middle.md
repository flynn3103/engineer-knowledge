# Tracking & Prioritizing — Middle

## Goal

Rank debt by expected interest, not by embarrassment or age.

## Prioritization inputs

- Cost of delay if the debt remains.
- Frequency of change in the affected area.
- Risk of defect, outage, or missed commitment.
- Size and reversibility of the repayment work.

## Workflow

1. Keep debt in the normal backlog or linked to roadmap work.
2. Review the highest-interest items regularly.
3. Bundle paydown with work that already touches the area.
4. Close or re-estimate stale items.

## Use formulas carefully

WSJF can structure a conversation, but estimated numbers are not facts. Record assumptions.
