# Tracking & Prioritizing — Professional

## Goal

Manage debt as a funded portfolio alongside product delivery.

## Operating model

- Use a mix of fix-on-touch, planned investment, and capacity for urgent hotspots.
- Give major debt a single accountable owner.
- Review shared platform debt with every affected team.
- Retire zombie tickets that no longer have a credible cost or sponsor.

## Roadmap language

Describe the feature enabled, the interest avoided, and the measurable risk reduced. Do not ask for a blank “cleanup epic.”

## Reporting

Show decisions, trend, realized benefits, and remaining uncertainty. Escalate capacity conflicts instead of hiding them in engineering backlogs.
