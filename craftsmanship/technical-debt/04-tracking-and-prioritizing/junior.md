# Tracking & Prioritizing — Junior

## Goal

Turn a vague concern into a debt item that can be acted on.

## A useful debt ticket

- Problem and affected area.
- Current cost or risk.
- Likely future change that will be harder.
- Smallest safe next action.
- Owner and trigger for review.

## Prioritize simply

- High impact and low effort: do it soon.
- High impact and high effort: plan it with the feature or roadmap.
- Low impact: leave it visible but do not let it crowd out real work.

## Rule

A TODO in code is a reminder, not a plan. Link it to a tracked decision when the work matters.
