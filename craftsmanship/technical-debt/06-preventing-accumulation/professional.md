# Preventing Accumulation — Professional

## Goal

Align incentives, process, and tooling so teams can sustain quality.

## Organizational practices

- Fund prevention as delivery capacity, not optional polish.
- Reward early risk reporting and safe simplification.
- Preserve quality expectations during reorganizations, migrations, and deadline pressure.
- Give teams supported templates, libraries, and operational standards.

## Leadership check

If teams repeatedly take reckless shortcuts, inspect goals, staffing, ownership, and deadlines before blaming individual discipline.
