# What Is Technical Debt — Professional

## Goal

Explain debt in delivery and business terms so it can be managed as a portfolio.

## Translate the cost

- Longer lead time for a planned feature.
- Higher incident probability or recovery cost.
- Increased vendor, security, or compliance exposure.
- Reduced ability to hire, onboard, or change direction.

## Governance

- Name an owner for material debt.
- Distinguish strategic investment from tactical shortcuts.
- Require a visible decision and exit criteria for major debt.
- Tie repayment to roadmap work that will benefit from it.

## Avoid

- Funding cleanup with no outcome or boundary.
- Using debt language to hide an unprioritized engineering preference.
