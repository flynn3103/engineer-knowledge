# Technical Debt — Middle

Classify debt by intent and context: deliberate versus accidental, prudent versus reckless. The label explains origin, not current priority.

Estimate interest through extra engineering time, defects, incidents, review delay, dependency blocks, and opportunity loss. Compare remediation cost, expected future changes, failure impact, and learning value.

Choose a response: pay now, pay incrementally, contain behind a seam, monitor a trigger, or consciously accept. Tie remediation to an outcome and verify improvement.

## Test yourself

1. Which evidence estimates debt interest?
2. When is deliberate debt prudent?
3. How can a seam contain debt?
4. What outcome proves repayment helped?
