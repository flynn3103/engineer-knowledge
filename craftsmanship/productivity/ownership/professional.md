# Ownership — Professional

**Your question:** How do I build an organization where people own their work? How do I establish norms for ownership and accountability?

Professional level teaches you to build systems and culture that enable ownership across the organization. You're establishing norms, measuring ownership health, and creating an environment where people can own their work sustainably.

## Design and operations checklist

1. **Establish ownership norms.** What does ownership mean in your organization?
2. **Create commitment templates.** Provide templates for making clear commitments.
3. **Teach ownership skills.** Help people learn how to own their work.
4. **Measure ownership health.** Track whether ownership is working.
5. **Protect sustainable delivery.** Prevent burnout and unsustainable pressure.

## Ownership norms

Establish what ownership means:

| Norm | What it means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Clear commitments | Commit to outcomes, not effort | "I'm committing to fix the bug by Friday if QA can test by Thursday" |
| Honest communication | Communicate progress and issues early | "I found a blocker. Here's what I'm doing about it." |
| Scope definition | Be clear about what's in and out | "I'm committing to payments, not refunds" |
| Quality protection | Don't skip quality controls under pressure | "We'll test this even though we're in a hurry" |
| Trade-off visibility | Make trade-offs explicit | "If we do X, we can't do Y" |
| Accountability | Own outcomes, not just effort | "I'm accountable for delivering this feature" |

## Commitment template

Provide a template for making clear commitments:

```
Commitment:
- What: [What are you committing to?]
- Scope: [What's in? What's out?]
- Timeline: [When will you deliver?]
- Assumptions: [What must be true?]
- Risks: [What could go wrong?]
- Checkpoints: [How will you know you're on track?]

Conditions for renegotiation:
- If [condition], we'll need to renegotiate
- If [condition], we'll need to renegotiate
```

## Teaching ownership skills

Help people learn to own their work:

1. **Model ownership.** Own your work yourself.

2. **Give feedback on ownership.** "That was a great commitment. You were clear about scope and risks."

3. **Provide training.** Teach how to make clear commitments, negotiate scope, estimate accurately.

4. **Practice together.** Role-play making commitments.

5. **Reflect after delivery.** "What went well? What would you do differently?"

## Measuring ownership health

Track whether ownership is working:

| Metric | What it tells you | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Commitment accuracy | Are people's commitments realistic? | >80% of commitments are met |
| Scope clarity | Are people clear about scope? | Scope changes are rare and communicated early |
| Communication quality | Do people communicate progress? | Issues are raised early, not at deadline |
| Pressure sustainability | Is delivery sustainable? | <10% after-hours work, low burnout |
| Accountability | Do people own outcomes? | People follow through on commitments |
| Trust | Do people trust each other? | >80% trust in team surveys |

## Realistic scenario: Building ownership culture

**Current state (50 engineers):**
- People make vague commitments
- Issues are hidden until deadline
- Pressure is unsustainable
- People don't own outcomes
- Trust is low

**Target state (100 engineers):**
- People make clear commitments
- Issues are raised early
- Delivery is sustainable
- People own outcomes
- Trust is high

**Implementation plan:**

**Phase 1: Establish norms (Month 1-2)**
- Define ownership norms
- Create commitment template
- Share with the organization
- Get feedback and iterate

**Phase 2: Teach skills (Month 2-4)**
- Teach how to make clear commitments
- Teach how to negotiate scope
- Teach how to estimate accurately
- Teach how to communicate progress
- Practice together in team meetings

**Phase 3: Model and reinforce (Month 4-6)**
- Model ownership in your own work
- Give feedback on ownership
- Celebrate good ownership
- Address poor ownership

**Phase 4: Measure and improve (Ongoing)**
- Track ownership metrics
- Gather feedback from teams
- Iterate on norms and training
- Celebrate improvements

## Common mistakes at professional level

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No ownership norms | Everyone owns differently | Establish clear norms |
| Teaching without modeling | People don't believe the norms | Model ownership yourself |
| Not measuring ownership | You don't know if it's working | Track ownership metrics |
| Punishing missed commitments | People make vague commitments to be safe | Reward honest communication, not perfect delivery |
| Not protecting sustainable delivery | People burn out | Protect focus time and prevent overwork |
