# Communication — Professional

**Your question:** How do I build a communication culture where people communicate effectively? How do I teach communication skills? How do I measure communication health?

Senior level teaches you to facilitate communication and resolve conflicts. At professional level, you're building systems and culture that enable good communication across the organization. You're establishing norms, teaching skills, and measuring whether communication is working.

## Design and operations checklist

1. **Establish communication norms.** How should people communicate? What's expected?
2. **Create communication templates.** Provide templates for common communication scenarios (feedback, decisions, conflicts).
3. **Teach communication skills.** Help people learn how to communicate effectively.
4. **Measure communication health.** Track whether communication is working.
5. **Iterate on culture.** Gather feedback and improve.

## Communication norms

Establish how people should communicate:

| Scenario | Norm | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Giving feedback | Use SBI framework, focus on behavior | "In the code review, you wrote 'This is inefficient' without explaining. I felt defensive." |
| Making decisions | Explain options and trade-offs | "We're choosing PostgreSQL because we need consistency. The trade-off is harder horizontal scaling." |
| Disagreeing | Disagree respectfully, focus on ideas | "I see your point. I'm concerned about the complexity. What if we..." |
| Escalating | Escalate early, don't let problems fester | "I'm concerned about this. Can we discuss it?" |
| Listening | Listen to understand, not to respond | Ask questions, paraphrase, acknowledge |

## Communication templates

Provide templates for common scenarios:

**Feedback template (SBI):**
```
Situation: [When did this happen? What was the context?]
Behavior: [What specifically did the person do?]
Impact: [What was the result? How did it affect you or the team?]
```

**Decision communication template:**
```
Decision: [What did we decide?]
Reasoning: [Why did we make this decision?]
Options considered: [What other options did we consider?]
Trade-offs: [What are we gaining? What are we losing?]
Revisit date: [When will we revisit this decision?]
```

**Conflict resolution template:**
```
Perspective A: [What does side A want? Why?]
Perspective B: [What does side B want? Why?]
Common ground: [What do they both want?]
Resolution: [What's the path forward?]
```

## Teaching communication skills

Help people learn to communicate effectively:

1. **Model good communication.** Communicate clearly and honestly yourself.

2. **Give feedback on communication.** "That was clear and specific. I understood exactly what you meant."

3. **Provide training.** Teach frameworks like STAR, SBI, and active listening.

4. **Practice together.** Role-play difficult conversations.

5. **Celebrate good communication.** "That was a great way to handle that conflict."

## Measuring communication health

Track whether communication is working:

| Metric | What it tells you | Healthy signal |
|---|---|---|
| Feedback quality | Are people giving specific, constructive feedback? | Feedback is specific and focuses on behavior |
| Decision clarity | Do people understand decisions and the reasoning? | People can explain the trade-offs |
| Conflict resolution time | How long does it take to resolve conflicts? | Conflicts are resolved within days, not weeks |
| Escalation rate | How often do problems escalate? | Problems are addressed early, few escalations |
| Communication satisfaction | Do people feel heard and understood? | >80% satisfaction in surveys |
| Psychological safety | Do people feel safe to disagree? | People voice concerns and disagreements |

## Realistic scenario: Building communication culture

**Current state (50 engineers):**
- Feedback is vague and sometimes harsh
- Decisions are made without explaining reasoning
- Conflicts fester for weeks
- People don't feel safe to disagree
- Communication is a problem

**Target state (100 engineers):**
- Feedback is specific and constructive
- Decisions are explained with reasoning and trade-offs
- Conflicts are resolved within days
- People feel safe to disagree
- Communication is a strength

**Implementation plan:**

**Phase 1: Establish norms (Month 1-2)**
- Define communication norms
- Create communication templates
- Share with the organization
- Get feedback and iterate

**Phase 2: Teach skills (Month 2-4)**
- Teach STAR framework for explaining ideas
- Teach SBI framework for giving feedback
- Teach active listening
- Teach conflict resolution
- Practice together in team meetings

**Phase 3: Model and reinforce (Month 4-6)**
- Model good communication in your own interactions
- Give feedback on communication
- Celebrate good communication
- Address poor communication

**Phase 4: Measure and improve (Ongoing)**
- Track communication metrics
- Gather feedback from teams
- Iterate on norms and training
- Celebrate improvements

## Common mistakes at professional level

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No communication norms | Everyone communicates differently | Establish clear norms |
| Teaching without modeling | People don't believe the norms | Model good communication yourself |
| Not measuring communication | You don't know if it's working | Track communication metrics |
| Punishing disagreement | People don't voice concerns | Make it safe to disagree |
| Not addressing poor communication | Bad habits persist | Address poor communication early |
