# Pair and Mob Programming — Senior

## Goal

Design collaboration systems that reduce concentrated risk without wasting group capacity.

## Strategic use

- Mob on cross-cutting decisions with high reversal cost.
- Pair senior and newer engineers on real ownership transfers.
- Use incident swarming first, then convert discoveries into runbooks and tests.
- Inspect participation patterns; psychological safety is a delivery concern.

## Guardrails

- Do not substitute group coding for clear architecture or written decisions.
- Avoid permanent hero pairs that become another silo.
- Balance synchronous work against deep-focus time and remote constraints.
