# Pair and Mob Programming — Professional

## Goal

Use collaborative programming as an organizational learning and risk-management tool.

## Operating model

- Reserve it for high-leverage work: incidents, unfamiliar systems, critical design, and onboarding.
- Rotate across team and domain boundaries to reduce knowledge silos.
- Track outcomes: time to unblock, review rework, and spread of ownership.
- Respect accessibility, time zones, and focus needs; async collaboration remains valuable.

## Facilitate well

- Name a facilitator who protects the agenda and participation.
- Record decisions, assumptions, and follow-up owners.
- End when solo work becomes cheaper than group attention.
