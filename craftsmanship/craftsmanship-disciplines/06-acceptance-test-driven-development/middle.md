# Acceptance Test-Driven Development — Middle

## Goal

Use examples to discover rules, boundaries, and missing decisions before delivery.

## Example mapping

- List the story, its rules, and concrete examples together.
- Include happy paths, boundaries, errors, and policy exceptions.
- Mark unknowns as questions for the product owner.

## Automation choices

- Keep most business rules below the UI.
- Automate a few end-to-end journeys for confidence.
- Make step names describe intent, not clicks or selectors.
