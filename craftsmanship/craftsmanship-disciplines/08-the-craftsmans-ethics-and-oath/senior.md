# The Craftsperson's Ethics & Oath — Senior

## Goal

Lead responsibly when organizational pressure conflicts with user safety or professional duty.

## Escalation ladder

1. Document the concrete risk and evidence.
2. Propose a safer delivery path.
3. Escalate to accountable technical and business leaders.
4. Use formal reporting channels when internal escalation fails.
5. Refuse work that creates unacceptable harm.

## Leadership duties

- Design incentives that reward early risk reporting.
- Preserve decision records and dissent.
- Learn from failures without normalizing preventable harm.
- Seek legal, security, and domain expertise for high-stakes decisions.
