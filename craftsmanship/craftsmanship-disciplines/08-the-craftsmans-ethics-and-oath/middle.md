# The Craftsperson's Ethics & Oath — Middle

## Goal

Make responsible choices when quality, speed, privacy, and safety pull in different directions.

## A practical decision check

- Who could be harmed if this fails or is misused?
- What evidence supports shipping now?
- What guardrail, rollback, or disclosure reduces the risk?
- Who needs to know before the decision is made?

## Act constructively

- State the risk in plain language and offer options.
- Distinguish a reversible shortcut from an unsafe omission.
- Record decisions that affect users, data, or operations.
- Escalate through the agreed path when concerns remain.
