# The Craftsperson's Ethics & Oath — Professional

## Goal

Build team practices that make ethical action normal, visible, and supported.

## Team obligations

- Define escalation paths for security, privacy, reliability, and safety concerns.
- Run blameless reviews that still name systemic causes and owners.
- Give people authority to pause a release when evidence is insufficient.
- Make trade-offs explicit in planning and incident communication.

## Communication pattern

State the impact, evidence, uncertainty, options, and recommendation. Avoid both silent compliance and unsupported alarm.
