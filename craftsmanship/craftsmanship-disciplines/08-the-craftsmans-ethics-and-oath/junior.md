# The Craftsperson's Ethics & Oath — Junior

## Goal

Treat users, teammates, and future maintainers as people affected by your work.

## Daily responsibilities

- Be honest about what you know and what you have not checked.
- Protect private data and follow security practices.
- Raise risks early instead of hiding them behind a deadline.
- Leave code, tests, and notes easier for the next person.

## When unsure

1. Stop and gather facts.
2. Ask a teammate or lead.
3. Document the risk and the decision.
4. Escalate if harm could result.
