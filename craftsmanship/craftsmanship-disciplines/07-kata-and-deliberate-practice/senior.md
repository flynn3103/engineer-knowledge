# Kata and Deliberate Practice — Senior

## Goal

Create a learning system that improves judgment, not just mechanical speed.

## Leadership practices

- Build a skills map from operational and delivery evidence.
- Offer progressively harder exercises with coaching and reflection.
- Practice socio-technical skills: incident command, design review, and risk communication.
- Make learning safe; public failure is useful only when blame is absent.

## Guardrails

- Do not confuse training with a substitute for staffing or better tooling.
- Retire exercises that no longer reflect the team's real risks.
