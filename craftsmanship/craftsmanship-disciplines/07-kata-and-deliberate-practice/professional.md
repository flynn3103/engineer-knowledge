# Kata and Deliberate Practice — Professional

## Goal

Build team capability through intentional practice connected to real work.

## Program

- Set aside predictable, protected practice time.
- Rotate topics: testing, debugging, design, incidents, and communication.
- Use production-like constraints without exposing production data.
- Share short lessons and reusable techniques after each session.

## Evaluate

- Look for improved review quality, recovery time, and ownership spread.
- Do not rank people by kata speed or attendance.
- Change the curriculum when recurring delivery problems reveal a skill gap.
