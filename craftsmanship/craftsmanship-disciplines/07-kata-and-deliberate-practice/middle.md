# Kata and Deliberate Practice — Middle

## Goal

Turn repetition into measurable improvement rather than comfortable routine.

## Design a session

- Pick a specific skill and a baseline.
- Add a constraint: no debugger, smaller tests, or a different language.
- Get feedback from a peer, recording, or comparison with a reference solution.
- Keep a short log of hypothesis, result, and next focus.

## Avoid

- Repeating the same solution on autopilot.
- Measuring only speed.
- Practicing a technique without understanding when it applies.
