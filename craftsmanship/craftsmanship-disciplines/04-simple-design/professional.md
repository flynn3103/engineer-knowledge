# Simple Design — Professional

## Goal

Make simplicity a shared review and delivery standard.

## Team habits

- Review in rule order: correct, clear, non-duplicated, minimal.
- Keep APIs small and make defaults explicit.
- Record costly or irreversible design choices in an ADR.
- Use feature work to pay down complexity in the code it touches.

## Measure carefully

- Watch review time, change failure rate, and time to understand hotspots.
- Treat complexity scores as prompts for discussion, not a quota.
