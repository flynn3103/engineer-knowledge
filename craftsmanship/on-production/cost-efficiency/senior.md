# Cost Efficiency — Senior

Compare reserved, spot, serverless, and owned capacity using workload shape and interruption tolerance. Include performance economics: an optimization that halves CPU but doubles engineering complexity may not repay.

Model retention, compaction, replication, cross-region transfer, hardware acceleration, and carbon or power constraints. Use budgets and anomaly detection without incentivizing teams to hide necessary reliability cost.

## Test yourself

1. Which workload tolerates spot interruption?
2. How do you value engineering complexity?
3. Which replication cost protects a real objective?
4. What anomaly requires immediate investigation?
