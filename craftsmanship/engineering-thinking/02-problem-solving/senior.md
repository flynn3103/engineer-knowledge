# Problem-Solving — Senior

Senior work begins where the problem itself is disputed.

## Reduce ambiguity

Map stakeholders, outcomes, constraints, unknowns, and irreversible decisions. Use discovery work to retire the riskiest assumption first. A prototype answers feasibility; an experiment answers causality; an incremental release answers behavior under real use.

## Lead incidents and complex debugging

Separate mitigation from root-cause analysis. Stabilize the system, preserve evidence, assign investigation threads, maintain a shared timeline, and communicate uncertainty clearly. Avoid declaring root cause from correlation alone.

## Choose under trade-offs

Compare options using decision criteria agreed before advocacy: correctness, latency, recovery, operability, security, migration, ownership, and reversal cost. Record why rejected options lost.

## Test yourself

1. Which unknown should a spike retire first?
2. Why should mitigation and diagnosis be separate workstreams?
3. How do you prevent a loud stakeholder from choosing the criteria after seeing options?
4. What evidence upgrades a contributing factor to root cause?
