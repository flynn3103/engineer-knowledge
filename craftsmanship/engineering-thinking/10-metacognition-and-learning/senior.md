# Metacognition and Learning — Senior

Senior engineers must debug their own reasoning while decisions affect others.

Before a decision, record prediction, confidence, assumptions, alternatives, and disconfirming evidence. After outcomes arrive, score the process separately from luck. A good decision can have a bad outcome; repeated surprise signals a poor model or calibration.

Use premortems, dissent, teaching, and incident review to expose blind spots. Maintain an expertise boundary: what you know directly, infer from adjacent experience, or need a specialist to validate.

## Test yourself

1. How do you distinguish a bad decision from bad luck?
2. Which assumption is your model most sensitive to?
3. When should you defer to a specialist?
4. What recurring surprise reveals a calibration problem?
