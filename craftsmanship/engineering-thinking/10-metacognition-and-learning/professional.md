# Metacognition and Learning — Professional

Professional learning is a property of the organization, not a training calendar.

Knowledge must move through observable work: design reviews, pairing, incident analysis, communities of practice, internal documentation, rotations, and safe experiments. Reward people for revealing uncertainty and correcting the system, not for performing certainty.

## Design the learning system

- Map critical expertise and single-expert risks.
- Give practice environments realistic feedback.
- Capture decisions and incidents in searchable forms.
- Rotate responsibility without abandoning ownership.
- Measure time to competence, repeated incidents, review bottlenecks, and knowledge concentration.
- Fund maintenance of examples, runbooks, and training like production infrastructure.

```text
EXPERIENCE -> REFLECTION -> EXPLICIT MODEL -> PRACTICE -> TRANSFER
      psychological safety + feedback quality + institutional memory
```

## Test yourself

1. Which expertise creates succession risk in your organization?
2. How do you measure learning without encouraging credential theater?
3. What makes an incident review produce transferable knowledge?
4. How should platform teams teach without becoming permanent gatekeepers?
