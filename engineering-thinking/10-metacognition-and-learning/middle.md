# Metacognition and Learning — Middle

Deliberate practice targets a specific weakness just beyond current ability, provides fast feedback, and repeats with correction. “Build more projects” is experience; “diagnose five unfamiliar concurrency failures and explain each causal chain” is deliberate practice.

Build a knowledge map: concepts you can explain, skills you can demonstrate, assumptions not yet tested, and unknown areas. Use spaced retrieval for durable facts and varied application for transfer.

Seek feedback from tests, users, reviewers, profiles, incidents, and mentors. Distinguish outcome feedback (“it failed”) from process feedback (“you ignored contradictory evidence”).

## Test yourself

1. Turn a broad learning goal into deliberate practice.
2. Which feedback is fastest and most trustworthy?
3. How do spacing and variation serve different purposes?
4. What item belongs in your unknowns map?
