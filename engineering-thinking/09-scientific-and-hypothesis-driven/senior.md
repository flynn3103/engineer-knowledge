# Scientific Thinking — Senior

Senior experiments protect validity and production safety.

Watch selection bias, interference between groups, instrumentation changes, multiple comparisons, regression to the mean, and stopping when results look favorable. Statistical significance does not imply practical value.

Use canaries, feature flags, shadow traffic, guardrail metrics, staged exposure, and automatic rollback. For incidents where controlled experiments are unsafe, use triangulation across logs, traces, timelines, and counterfactual comparisons.

## Test yourself

1. How can users in treatment affect control users?
2. Why is repeated peeking dangerous?
3. Which guardrail should stop a rollout?
4. How can observational incident evidence support causality?
