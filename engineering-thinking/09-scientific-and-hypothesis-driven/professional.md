# Scientific Thinking — Professional

At professional level, create trustworthy experimentation infrastructure and norms.

Standardize assignment, exposure logging, metric definitions, power checks, guardrails, analysis, and result publication. Track negative and inconclusive results to reduce publication bias. Require experiments to name the decision they inform.

## Checklist

- Is the claim falsifiable and decision-relevant?
- Are randomization and analysis units correct?
- Can treatment leak into control?
- Are metrics stable, sensitive, and difficult to game?
- Are operational, security, and customer guardrails enforced?
- Is the result reproducible and discoverable?

```text
QUESTION -> PRE-REGISTER -> ASSIGN -> EXPOSE -> MEASURE -> ANALYZE -> DECIDE
             causal validity + operational safety + institutional memory
```

## Test yourself

1. Design governance for a company-wide experimentation platform.
2. How do you detect metric drift and sample-ratio mismatch?
3. When is an A/B test unethical or operationally unsafe?
4. How should the organization learn from null results?
