# Creative and Lateral Thinking — Professional

Innovation depends on an organizational system: psychological safety, slack, diverse perspectives, customer access, fast experiments, and honest termination of weak ideas.

Avoid innovation theater. Fund explicit hypotheses and learning milestones, not slide decks. Separate discovery metrics from delivery metrics. Preserve local autonomy while sharing reusable evidence.

## Checklist

1. Frame the opportunity without embedding a solution.
2. Include affected disciplines and users.
3. Generate options across removal, movement, substitution, inversion, and combination.
4. Fund a portfolio by uncertainty and upside.
5. Bound experiments and publish learning.
6. Scale only after mechanism and operations are credible.

```text
OPPORTUNITY -> DIVERSE OPTIONS -> BOUNDED BETS -> EVIDENCE -> SCALE OR STOP
                slack + safety + constraints + customer reality
```

## Test yourself

1. Which incentives suppress unconventional but useful ideas?
2. How should discovery funding differ from delivery funding?
3. What evidence justifies scaling a prototype?
4. How do you prevent a platform team from centralizing creativity?
