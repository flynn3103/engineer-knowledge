# Probabilistic Thinking — Professional

Professional judgment allocates finite risk across reliability, security, product bets, and migrations.

Set explicit risk appetite and decision thresholds. Combine outside-view base rates with inside-view evidence. Prefer a portfolio of bounded experiments over one irreversible forecast when uncertainty is high.

## Checklist

- What is the reference class and its selection bias?
- Which variables are dependent or heavy-tailed?
- What loss is unacceptable regardless of expected value?
- What is reversible, hedgeable, insurable, or isolatable?
- Which evidence has the highest value of information?
- How will forecasts be scored and recalibrated?

```text
PRIOR + EVIDENCE -> DISTRIBUTION -> CONSEQUENCES -> ACTION
        dependence | tails | reversibility | learning value
```

## Test yourself

1. Design a risk portfolio for three platform migrations.
2. How would you detect overconfidence in architecture forecasts?
3. When should a low-probability risk block launch?
4. How does correlated risk change redundancy strategy?
