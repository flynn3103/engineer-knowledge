# Critical Thinking — Middle

Middle-level decisions need competing hypotheses. Generate at least two explanations before investigating. Seek discriminating evidence rather than more evidence consistent with your favorite theory.

Common biases include confirmation bias, sunk-cost thinking, availability bias after a recent incident, anchoring on the first estimate, and survivorship bias from studying only successful systems.

Use a decision table with criteria defined before scoring. Distinguish measurements from predictions and preferences. Record confidence and missing evidence.

## Test yourself

1. How would you test whether a recent deployment caused an outage?
2. Which bias appears when a team continues a migration because it already spent a year?
3. Why define criteria before comparing tools?
4. What evidence discriminates load from data corruption as a cause?
