# Critical Thinking — Professional

At professional level, design institutions that resist groupthink without creating paralysis.

Use independent review for high-blast-radius decisions, explicit dissent in decision records, evidence standards proportional to risk, and review dates tied to leading indicators. Protect messengers who reveal inconvenient data.

## Decision-quality checklist

- Are claim, evidence, assumption, inference, and value judgment distinguishable?
- Were alternatives generated before criteria hardened?
- Is missing or contradictory evidence visible?
- Did authority influence the conclusion beyond expertise relevance?
- Are conflicts of interest and incentives disclosed?
- Is there a trigger to reverse or revisit the decision?

```text
CLAIM + EVIDENCE + WARRANT + LIMITS + VALUES -> DECISION
             preserve dissent | audit outcomes | update beliefs
```

## Test yourself

1. Design an evidence standard for adopting a security-critical platform.
2. How do you preserve minority views without blocking every decision?
3. Which outcome data evaluates decision quality rather than luck?
4. How can metrics themselves become biased evidence?
