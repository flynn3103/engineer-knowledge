# Critical Thinking — Senior

Senior decisions combine incomplete evidence, competing values, and unequal failure costs.

Use premortems and red-team reviews to expose hidden assumptions. Invite the strongest version of opposing views. Separate reversible experiments from commitments with high migration, compliance, or data-loss cost.

Present a decision with context, alternatives, evidence, uncertainty, value trade-offs, failure modes, and review date. Do not hide judgment behind a weighted score; explain why weights matter.

## Test yourself

1. Which assumption deserves a premortem scenario?
2. How do asymmetric failure costs change the evidence threshold?
3. What makes a red-team review productive rather than political?
4. When should a decision be revisited?
